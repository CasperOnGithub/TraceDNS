TraceDNS is a DNS and infrastructure mapping tool written in Python. (YAY!)

It is designed to help investigate publicly avaliable DNS information about
domains and understand how their VISIBLE infrastructure is connected.

TraceDNS can be useful for:

- OSINT investigations
- Security research
- Domain infrastructure analysis
- Phishing investigations
- Understanding mail infrastructure
- Mapping publicly visible DNS relationships
- Learning how domains and their infrastructure are connected

TraceDNS is purely a reconnasaince tool, and therefore it does not have any abilities
that can be considered a grey-area or outright illegal. No port scans, no attempting 
access where you aren't belonged. That is not what it's used for.

Backstory of TraceDNS:

Pretty simple backstory to be fair, received a 419 scam email and decided to investigate for about 7 hours.
Didn't really end up finding the person behind it, which also originally wasn't really the goal. However on this
search I found it so annoying that I had to switch tabs constantly to check new information, and took a long time.
That is why I decided to make TraceDNS that makes my life a little bit easier.
